## 💬 Copywriting Strategy Used

### AIDA Framework Applied:
1. **Attention:** "Your Trusted Services, One Tap Away" (bold headline)
2. **Interest:** Specific benefits, provider counts, real scenarios
3. **Desire:** Trust signals, testimonials, limited offers
4. **Action:** Clear CTAs with action words

### Benefits Over Features:
- Not just "verified profiles" → "100% Verified Pros with background checks"
- Not just "messaging" → "Chat Before You Book - get quotes first"
- Not just "flexible pricing" → "Set Your Own Rates - keep more earnings"
