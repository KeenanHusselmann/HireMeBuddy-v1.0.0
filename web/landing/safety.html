Safety Guidelines

HireMeBuddy - Your safety is our priority

← Back to Home

HireMeBuddy is committed to providing a safe platform for clients and service providers. Please follow these safety guidelines to protect yourself and others.

For Clients: Booking Safely

Before Booking

  • Check Provider Verification: Look for the verified badge on provider profiles
  • Read Reviews: Review ratings and feedback from previous clients
  • Check Portfolio: View work samples and experience
  • Message First: Communicate via in-app chat before booking
  • Ask Questions: Clarify scope of work, pricing, and timeline

During Service

  • Stay Home: Be present during service provision when possible
  • Secure Valuables: Keep personal items and valuables secure
  • Use In-App Chat: Keep all communication within the app for your protection
  • Trust Your Instincts: If something feels wrong, it probably is
  • Emergency Contacts: Keep emergency numbers accessible
WARNING - Red Flags: Cancel immediately and report if a provider:
  • Asks to communicate outside the app
  • Requests payment outside the platform
  • Makes you feel uncomfortable or unsafe
  • Shows up intoxicated or unprofessional

For Providers: Working Safely

Before Accepting Bookings

  • Complete Profile: Add verification documents and portfolio
  • Clear Communication: Discuss job details and expectations upfront
  • Screen Clients: Read client reviews and booking history
  • Confirm Details: Verify location, time, and scope of work
  • Set Boundaries: Only accept jobs you're comfortable with

During Service

  • Share Location: Let someone know where you're working
  • Professional Conduct: Maintain professionalism at all times
  • Use Safety Equipment: Wear appropriate protective gear
  • Document Work: Take before/after photos when appropriate
  • Trust Your Instincts: Leave if you feel unsafe
WARNING - Warning Signs: Consider canceling if a client:
  • Has no reviews or consistently poor ratings
  • Requests services outside your expertise
  • Makes inappropriate comments or requests
  • Asks you to work in unsafe conditions

Payment Safety

  • Transparent Pricing: Agree on rates before starting work
  • Written Agreements: Use in-app chat to document terms
  • Secure Payments: Keep payment transactions within the platform when possible
  • Payment Disputes: Report payment issues to our support team
  • Receipts: Keep records of all transactions

Privacy and Data Protection

  • Personal Information: Don't share unnecessary personal details
  • Location Privacy: Only share exact addresses when booking is confirmed
  • Social Media: Don't feel obligated to connect on social platforms
  • Passwords: Never share your account credentials
  • Phishing: Be wary of suspicious messages or links

Communication Guidelines

  • Use In-App Chat: All messages are monitored for safety
  • Professional Language: Keep conversations respectful
  • No Harassment: Report abusive or inappropriate messages immediately
  • Spam Protection: Don't share promotional content or spam

COVID-19 and Health Safety

  • Follow current health guidelines and regulations
  • Wear masks if required or requested
  • Maintain social distancing when possible
  • Postpone bookings if you feel unwell
  • Sanitize hands and equipment regularly

Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unsafe behavior:

  • In-App Reporting: Use the report button on user profiles
  • Support Team: Contact safety@hiremebuddy.app
  • Emergency: Call local emergency services (999 in Namibia)
  • Evidence: Take screenshots of inappropriate messages
  • Immediate Action: We investigate all reports within 24 hours

What We Do to Keep You Safe

  • Provider Verification: Document and identity checks
  • Review System: Verified reviews from real bookings
  • 24/7 Support: Safety team available around the clock
  • Secure Platform: Encrypted communications and data
  • Account Monitoring: Suspicious activity detection
  • User Bans: Permanent removal of violators

Emergency Contacts

  • Emergency Services (Namibia): 999
  • Police: 10111
  • HireMeBuddy Safety: safety@hiremebuddy.app
  • General Support: info@hiremebuddy.app

Safety is a shared responsibility. Together, we build a trusted community.
