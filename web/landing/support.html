Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up?

Download the HireMeBuddy app from Google Play Store and follow the registration steps. Choose whether you're signing up as a client or service provider.

How do I book a service?

Browse providers by service category, view their profiles and reviews, then send a booking request with your preferred date and time.

How do I become a verified provider?

Complete your profile with professional information, upload verification documents (ID, certificates), and add portfolio samples. Our team will review and verify your profile within 24-48 hours.

What if I need to cancel a booking?

You can cancel bookings through the app. Please provide reasonable notice to avoid cancellation fees. Check the provider's cancellation policy before booking.

How do payments work?

Payments are arranged directly between clients and providers. Ensure all payment terms are agreed upon before service begins.

How do I report a problem?

Use the in-app report button on user profiles, or email safety@hiremebuddy.app for urgent safety concerns.
