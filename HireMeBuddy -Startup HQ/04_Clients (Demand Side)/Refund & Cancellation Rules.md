# Refund & Cancellation Rules

## Cancellation Policy

### Client Cancellations

#### More than 24 hours before booking
- Full refund
- No cancellation fee

#### 12-24 hours before booking
- 50% refund
- 50% cancellation fee

#### Less than 12 hours before booking
- No refund
- 100% cancellation fee

#### After service started
- No refund
- Full charge applies

### Provider Cancellations

#### More than 24 hours before booking
- Client receives full refund
- Provider receives warning

#### Less than 24 hours before booking
- Client receives full refund
- Provider penalty applied
- Strike against provider account

## Refund Policy

### Eligible for Full Refund
- Provider no-show
- Service not as described
- Safety concerns
- Provider cancellation

### Eligible for Partial Refund
- Incomplete service (prorated)
- Service quality issues (case-by-case)
- Late cancellation by client

### Not Eligible for Refund
- Client no-show
- Late cancellation by client (<12 hours)
- Service completed as agreed
- Client changed mind after completion

## Processing Time
- Refunds processed within 5-7 business days
- Credited to original payment method

## Special Circumstances
- Weather emergencies: Full refund, no penalty
- Medical emergencies: Case-by-case review
- Force majeure: Full refund, no penalty
