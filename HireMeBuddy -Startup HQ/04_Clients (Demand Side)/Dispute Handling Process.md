# Dispute Handling Process

## Types of Disputes

### Service Quality Issues
- Work not completed as agreed
- Poor quality of service
- Incomplete work

### Payment Disputes
- Overcharging
- Unauthorized charges
- Refund requests

### No-Show Situations
- Provider didn't arrive
- Client wasn't available

### Safety Concerns
- Inappropriate behavior
- Property damage
- Safety violations

## Dispute Resolution Steps

### Step 1: Initial Contact (0-24 hours)
1. Client/Provider reports issue via app
2. Provide details and evidence
3. Upload photos/documentation if applicable

### Step 2: Review (24-48 hours)
1. Support team reviews case
2. Contact both parties
3. Gather all relevant information

### Step 3: Resolution (48-72 hours)
1. Support team makes decision
2. Communicate outcome to both parties
3. Process refunds/adjustments if applicable

### Step 4: Follow-up
1. Ensure both parties satisfied
2. Implement corrective actions
3. Update policies if needed

## Evidence Required
- Photos/videos
- Chat messages
- Booking details
- Completion reports
- Payment receipts

## Resolution Options
- Full refund
- Partial refund
- Re-booking with another provider
- Provider warning/suspension
- Case closed - no action
