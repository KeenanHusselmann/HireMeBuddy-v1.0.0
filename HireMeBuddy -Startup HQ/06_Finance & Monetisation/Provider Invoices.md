# Provider Invoices

## Invoice Generation

### Automatic Invoice Creation
- Generated after each completed booking
- Sent to provider's registered email
- Available in provider dashboard

Financial Breakdown:
- Total Booking Amount: $[X]
- Platform Commission ([Y]%): -$[Z]

## Batch Invoicing
- Weekly summary invoice
- Monthly summary invoice
- Includes all bookings for period
- Shows total earnings and deductions

- Tax reporting ready
