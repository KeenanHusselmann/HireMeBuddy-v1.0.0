## Decisions

### Decision #1: Separate Client and Provider APKs
**Date**: December 13, 2025
**Decision Maker(s)**: Development Team
**Context**: Need to differentiate client and provider apps for user clarity
**Options Considered**:
1. Single app with role selection - Confusing for users
2. Separate APKs with different icons - Clear differentiation
**Decision**: Build separate APKs with distinct branding (teal for client, orange for provider)
**Rationale**: Clearer user experience, easier marketing, prevents confusion
**Impact**: Separate app store listings, clearer positioning
**Review Date**: After user testing

### Decision #2: Job Completion Feature
**Date**: December 2025
**Decision Maker(s)**: Product Team
**Context**: Need transparency in service delivery
**Decision**: Implement job completion reports submitted by providers
**Rationale**: Builds trust, provides documentation, improves accountability
**Impact**: Enhanced client confidence, better dispute resolution
**Review Date**: Q1 2026
