## Skill Verification

### Level 1: Basic
- Self-declared skills
- Portfolio required
- Client reviews

### Level 2: Verified
- Third-party verification
- Certifications uploaded
- Background check

### Level 3: Expert
- 50+ completed jobs
- 4.5+ average rating
- Advanced certifications
