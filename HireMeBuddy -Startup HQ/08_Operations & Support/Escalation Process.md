# Escalation Process

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1: Support Agent
- First point of contact
- Handles routine inquiries
- Standard issues
- Response time: 2-24 hours

### Level 2: Support Team Lead
- Complex issues
- Customer complaints
- Policy clarifications
- Response time: 24-48 hours

### Level 3: Operations Manager
- High-value disputes
- Multiple complaint cases
- Process improvements
- Response time: 48-72 hours

### Level 4: Executive Team
- Legal matters
- PR/Media issues
- Major safety concerns
- Platform-wide issues
- Response time: 72 hours

## When to Escalate

### Immediate Escalation (Level 3-4)
- Safety threats
- Legal notices
- Media inquiries
- Platform security issues
- Data breaches

### Standard Escalation (Level 2)
- Unresolved after 48 hours
- Customer extremely dissatisfied
- Policy violation unclear
- Refund amount >$[X]
- Provider with multiple complaints

### Agent-Initiated Escalation
When agent:
- Needs policy clarification
- Lacks authority to resolve
- Identifies systemic issue
- Feels uncomfortable with decision

## Escalation Process

### Step 1: Attempt Resolution at Current Level
- Use all available resources
- Consult knowledge base
- Follow SOPs
- Document attempts

### Step 2: Prepare Escalation
**Required Information:**
- Case summary
- Actions taken
- Why escalation needed
- Urgency level
- Proposed resolution

### Step 3: Submit Escalation
- Use internal ticketing system
- CC team lead
- Mark priority
- Set due date

### Step 4: Handover
- Brief receiving party
- Provide all documentation
- Remain available for questions
- Monitor progress

### Step 5: Resolution & Learning
- Document outcome
- Update knowledge base
- Share learnings with team
- Improve processes

## Escalation Template

```
ESCALATION REQUEST

Case ID: [ID]
Current Level: [1/2/3]
Escalating To: [Level/Name]
Priority: [Critical/High/Medium]

Original Issue:
[Brief description]

Actions Taken:
- [Action 1]
- [Action 2]
- [Action 3]

Reason for Escalation:
[Explanation]

Customer Sentiment:
[Satisfied/Neutral/Frustrated/Angry]

Proposed Resolution:
[Your recommendation]

Submitted by: [Name]
Date: [Date]
```

## De-escalation Techniques
Before escalating, try:
- Active listening
- Empathy statements
- Offering alternatives
- Involving team lead informally
- Explaining process clearly

## Communication Guidelines

### When Escalating to Customer:
```
"I want to ensure you receive the best possible resolution. 
I'm connecting you with [Title] who specializes in these situations. 
They will contact you within [timeframe]."
```

### Internal Communication:
- Clear and concise
- All relevant facts
- Objective tone
- Specific requests
